    Recovery passphrase

    Encrypts exports and lets saved credentials move to a new host. Keep it with the proxy's documentation: without it an export cannot be imported. At least 12 characters.

    Export

    Downloads a zip of every user, server, assignment, saved credential and sign-in session, encrypted with the recovery passphrase.

    Frozen, the proxy keeps signing users in and carrying sessions, but refuses new saved credentials and admin changes, so nothing made after the export is lost.
